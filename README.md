# 💧 ResourceLoop
### Smart Water Recycling & Energy Management System

ResourceLoop is an innovative smart water reuse system designed for large buildings and facilities. It collects condensate water from air conditioning and HVAC systems, monitors and treats the collected water, and enables its reuse for non-potable applications such as irrigation, toilet flushing, and cleaning.

The project aims to reduce water waste, improve resource efficiency, and support smarter energy management through monitoring and data-driven insights.
